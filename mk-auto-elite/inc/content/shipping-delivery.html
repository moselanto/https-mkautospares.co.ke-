Shipping & Delivery Policy

MK Auto Spares delivers vehicle parts and accessories across Kenya. This policy explains how we process, pack and deliver your order, expected timelines and costs.

Order Processing

Orders are processed Monday to Saturday, 8:00am to 6:00pm (excluding Sundays and public holidays). We confirm price, stock and fitment with you on WhatsApp before dispatch. In-stock items are typically dispatched within 1 business day of confirmed payment.

Delivery Timelines

  • Nairobi and environs: same day or next day, 1–2 business days.
  • Major towns (Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Nyeri, Thika, etc.): 1–3 business days.
  • Other regions: 2–5 business days, depending on courier schedules.

Timelines are estimates from the point of dispatch and may vary with courier availability, weather, and location.

Delivery Methods

  • Courier & parcel services to towns countrywide (established bus/matatu parcel services and courier companies).
  • Rider delivery within Nairobi for urgent orders.
  • Pickup from our shop along Kirinyaga Road, Nairobi (Glogon, behind the Shell petrol station, near Kanju washrooms) during business hours — free.

Delivery Costs

Delivery is charged based on destination, parcel weight and size, and the courier used. We confirm the exact delivery cost with you before dispatch so there are no surprises. Bulky or heavy items (e.g. radiators, body panels, nose cuts) may attract higher courier fees.

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery is available within selected areas of Nairobi, subject to confirmation. For up-country deliveries, we typically require payment (M-Pesa or bank) before dispatch, or payment to the courier on collection where the service allows.

Checking Your Order on Arrival

Please inspect your parcel at the point of delivery or collection. If the item is damaged in transit or incorrect, contact us the same day on WhatsApp with photos so we can resolve it quickly under our Return & Refund Policy.

Tracking

Where the courier provides tracking, we share the tracking number or waybill with you on WhatsApp after dispatch.
