Prefer to check with a person?

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and we will confirm your order status, dispatch time and delivery window. For parts bought over WhatsApp or by phone, quoting the reference we sent you is the fastest way for us to find your order.

Delivery within Nairobi is typically same or next day. Countrywide delivery via courier or parcel service usually arrives within 1 to 3 business days depending on your location.
