Payment Methods

We make paying simple and secure. Choose the option that suits you best. All prices are in Kenya Shillings (KES).

M-Pesa (Lipa na M-Pesa – Pay Bill)

Pay conveniently via Safaricom M-Pesa Pay Bill:

  1. Open M-Pesa, then select Lipa na M-Pesa.
  2. Choose Pay Bill.
  3. Enter Business Number 529914.
  4. Enter Account Number 4268.
  5. Enter the amount, enter your M-Pesa PIN, and confirm.

After paying, keep your M-Pesa confirmation SMS and share it with us on WhatsApp so we can match it to your order. Always confirm the business name shown before you approve the payment; if anything looks different, contact us on our official number first.

Cash

Pay in cash when you buy or collect at our shop along Kirinyaga Road, Nairobi. Cash on Delivery is available within selected areas of Nairobi, subject to confirmation. For up-country orders, payment is usually made before dispatch or to the courier on collection, depending on the service.

Bank Transfer

Bank and mobile-banking transfers are accepted for larger or special-order items. Bank details are provided on request when you place your order.

Order Confirmation

Because some items are listed as “Price on request”, we confirm the final price, delivery cost and payment method with you on WhatsApp before you pay. This ensures you always know the total before committing.

Security

We only use trusted payment channels. If you ever receive payment instructions that look unusual, contact us directly on our official number +254 702 343 302 before paying.
