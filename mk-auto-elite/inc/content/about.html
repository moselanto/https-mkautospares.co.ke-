Who We Are

MK Auto Spares is a Nairobi-based supplier of vehicle spare parts and accessories, serving motorists, mechanics and garages across Kenya. We bring three things together under one roof: quality ex-used (used / ex-Japan) parts, brand-new genuine parts, and an in-house service centre where our team can fit what you buy and keep your car on the road.

We stock parts for the most common makes on Kenyan roads — Toyota, Nissan, Mazda, Subaru, Honda, Mitsubishi, Suzuki, Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Lexus, Isuzu, Audi and more — covering engine components, electrical and electronic modules, body parts, lighting, cooling, fuel systems, transmission and everyday accessories.

What We Offer

  • Quality ex-used parts — carefully selected used and ex-Japan parts, tested and inspected so you get dependable quality at a fair price.
  • Brand-new genuine parts — new OEM and quality aftermarket parts sourced from trusted suppliers.
  • Service centre & fitment — our workshop can diagnose, fit and service, so the part you buy is installed correctly.
  • Fitment guidance — use our vehicle finder or send us your make, model and year and we confirm compatibility before you buy.
  • Fast countrywide delivery — through trusted courier partners to all major towns in Kenya.

Why Customers Choose Us

We combine a searchable catalogue with genuine human support and a real workshop. Whether you need a control module for a Toyota Prado, a nose cut for a Mazda Axela, an alternator for a Nissan X-Trail, or a full service, we help you confirm the exact part the first time so you avoid costly wrong purchases — and we can fit it for you if you wish.

Our Commitment

We stand behind what we sell. Ex-used parts are inspected before they leave us, eligible parts carry a warranty, our prices are transparent, and our returns process is straightforward. If something is not right, we make it right.
