Return & Refund Policy

We want you to be confident in every purchase from MK Auto Spares. If a part is faulty, incorrect, or not as described, you may return it under the terms below. This policy is in addition to your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2012 (Kenya).

Return Window

You may request a return within 7 days of receiving your item. To be eligible, the part must be unused, uninstalled, in its original condition and packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase (order reference, receipt or M-Pesa confirmation).

What Can Be Returned

  • Parts that are defective or damaged on arrival.
  • Parts that do not match the item described or ordered.
  • Wrong parts supplied by us due to our error — returned at no cost to you.

What Cannot Be Returned

  • Electrical and electronic parts (ECUs, control modules, sensors, injectors) that have been installed, wired, or tampered with, unless proven faulty on arrival.
  • Items damaged by incorrect installation, misuse, or normal wear.
  • Special-order items, once confirmed and dispatched, unless faulty.

How to Start a Return

  1. Contact us on WhatsApp or email info@mkautospares.co.ke within 7 days, quoting your order reference and describing the issue.
  2. Where helpful, send clear photos or a short video showing the fault or mismatch.
  3. We will confirm whether the item qualifies and share return instructions.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned part, we notify you of the outcome. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method (usually to M-Pesa). Where you prefer, we can issue a replacement or store credit instead of a refund.

Return Shipping Costs

If the return is due to our error or a faulty part, we cover the return shipping. For change-of-mind or ordering errors on the customer’s side, the customer is responsible for return shipping, and the original delivery fee is non-refundable.

Warranty Claims

Parts sold with a warranty are covered against manufacturing defects for the stated period. Warranty claims require proof of purchase and may require inspection. Warranty does not cover damage from incorrect fitting, accidents, or misuse.
