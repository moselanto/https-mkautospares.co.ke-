A car battery starts your engine and powers everything electrical. Choosing the right one and spotting a failing battery early saves you from being stranded.

Choosing the right battery

The right battery matches your car's required size, terminal layout, and capacity (measured in amp-hours and cranking amps). Fitting a battery that is too small leaves you short of power; the wrong size may not fit the tray. Give us your car details and we will match the correct battery.

Signs your battery is failing

  • Slow or laboured engine cranking, especially in the morning.
  • Dashboard warning lights or dim headlights at idle.
  • Electronics resetting or behaving oddly.
  • A battery more than a few years old that struggles after short trips.

Making a battery last

Short trips never fully recharge a battery, so an occasional longer drive helps. Keep the terminals clean and tight, and have the charging system checked if a new battery keeps going flat, because the fault may be the alternator rather than the battery.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a car battery last? It varies with use and climate; have it tested regularly once it is a few years old.
