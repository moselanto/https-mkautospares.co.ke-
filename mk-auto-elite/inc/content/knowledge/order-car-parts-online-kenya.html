More Kenyans now order car parts online instead of spending a day on Kirinyaga Road. Done right, it is faster, cheaper and safer. Here is how to order the right part online and pay safely with M-Pesa.

Step 1: identify your car exactly

Your chassis number (on the logbook and in the engine bay) tells us the exact model, engine and year. Use our vehicle finder or send the chassis number on WhatsApp. Read how to find parts by chassis number.

Step 2: choose the part and grade

Pick genuine, quality aftermarket or ex-Japan, depending on your budget. We explain the difference before you pay.

Step 3: pay safely

  • Pay by M-Pesa to our business Paybill, never to a personal number.
  • Card and bank transfer are also accepted.
  • For larger repairs, ask about Lipa Mdogo Mdogo.
  • You get a receipt by e-mail and WhatsApp.

Step 4: delivery or collection

  • Nairobi: same-day delivery to your home, office or mechanic.
  • Countrywide: by courier to Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Nyeri, Meru, Thika and other towns.
  • Collection: pick up from our shop on Kirinyaga Road.

Returns and warranty

Check the part before your mechanic fits it. If it is the wrong part, contact us quickly. See our returns policy for details.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to buy car parts online in Kenya? Yes, if you buy from a real shop with a physical address, pay to a business Paybill and get a receipt.

How long does countrywide delivery take? Usually one to two working days to major towns, depending on the courier.

Can my mechanic order for me? Yes. Send us the part list and the chassis number and we will deliver to the garage.
