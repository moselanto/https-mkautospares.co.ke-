The Honda Fit, including the Fit Hybrid and the Fit Shuttle, is popular in Kenya for its space and fuel economy. Here are the issues owners search for most and how to fix them.

1. Hybrid battery faults

Older Fit Hybrids may show a hybrid system warning when the battery ages. Testing and cell replacement can often save the pack. See our hybrid battery price guide.

2. Dual-clutch gearbox jerking (Fit Hybrid)

The dual-clutch gearbox on many Fit Hybrids can jerk at low speed or when moving off. Software updates and correct maintenance often help. Have it checked by a Honda-experienced mechanic before replacing parts.

3. CVT whine (petrol Fit)

A whining noise or slipping usually means old CVT fluid. Use only the Honda CVT fluid specified for your car. See our CVT gearbox guide.

4. Suspension noises

Front stabilizer links and shocks wear on rough roads, causing knocking. They are affordable to replace.

5. Air conditioning not cooling

A common complaint in hot weather. Usually low gas from a leak, a blocked cabin filter or a failing compressor clutch. See car AC not cooling.

6. 12V battery problems

Hybrids use a small 12V battery that powers the computers. A weak one causes strange warning lights and starting faults.
