The Toyota Probox and Succeed are Kenya's working cars: delivery vans, taxis, farm runners and family cars. They carry heavy loads every day, so certain parts wear faster. This guide helps owners and fleet managers keep them on the road with less downtime.

Parts that wear fastest

  • Rear leaf springs and shocks: overloading sags the rear and wears the shocks. Replace sagging springs before they crack.
  • Front lower arms, ball joints and links: knocking over bumps is the usual sign.
  • Brake pads and rear shoes: heavy loads mean more braking. Check them every service.
  • Clutch kit (manual models): a slipping clutch or a high biting point means it is due.
  • Wheel bearings: a humming noise that rises with speed.

Engine care

Probox engines are tough but need regular oil, filters and spark plugs. Overheating is often caused by an old radiator or a failing thermostat in town traffic. Read our service schedule.

Fleet tips

  • Keep a simple service log for every vehicle.
  • Keep fast-moving parts (filters, pads, links) in stock to avoid downtime.
  • Ask us about fleet pricing and account billing.

Frequently asked questions

Are Probox and Succeed parts the same? Most mechanical parts are shared. Body parts and some trims differ, so use your chassis number for an exact match.

How often should a Probox be serviced? About every 5,000 km for heavy commercial use, or 5,000 to 10,000 km for normal use, depending on the oil.
