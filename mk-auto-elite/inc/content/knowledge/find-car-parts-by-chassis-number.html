What is a chassis number?

The chassis or frame number is a code stamped by the manufacturer that identifies your vehicle's exact model, generation, and often the engine and trim. On Japanese imports it usually looks like a few letters and digits (for example NZE161 or RU1), sometimes with a serial number after a dash.

Where to find it

  • On the vehicle registration or import documents.
  • Stamped on a metal plate in the engine bay or on the body.
  • On the manufacturer's build plate inside the door frame or under the bonnet.

Why it matters for parts

Two cars that look identical can use different parts depending on the generation, engine, or facelift. The chassis code removes the guesswork, which is especially important for suspension, engine, and electrical parts where the wrong version simply will not fit or work.

Is the chassis number the same as the number plate? No. The number plate is your registration; the chassis number identifies the vehicle's build.
