Regular servicing is the cheapest way to keep a car reliable and hold its value. The exact intervals depend on your vehicle, so always follow your manufacturer's schedule, but here is what good maintenance looks like for Kenyan conditions.

Every service

  • Change engine oil and the oil filter with the correct grade.
  • Check and replace the air and cabin filters as needed, more often on dusty roads.
  • Inspect brakes, tyres, and suspension for wear.
  • Check all fluid levels: coolant, brake, power steering, and transmission.

Periodic items

  • Fuel filter and spark plugs at the recommended intervals.
  • Timing belt replacement on belt-driven engines, per the manufacturer schedule.
  • Transmission fluid, especially for CVTs, at the correct intervals.
  • Coolant and brake fluid renewal over time.

Kenya-specific tips

Dust and rough roads are hard on filters, suspension, and cooling. Shorten filter and inspection intervals slightly in very dusty areas, and address knocks and leaks early before they become bigger repairs.
