Your battery starts the car, but the alternator keeps it running and recharges the battery as you drive. When the charging system fails, the car will eventually stall no matter how good the battery is.

What the charging system does

The alternator generates electricity while the engine runs, powering the lights, electronics, and ignition, and topping up the battery. A drive belt turns the alternator, and a voltage regulator keeps the output steady.

Signs of a failing alternator

  • The battery warning light glowing while driving.
  • Dimming or flickering headlights, especially at idle.
  • Electrical items behaving erratically.
  • A repeatedly flat battery even after replacement.
  • A whining noise or burning smell from the belt area.

Battery or alternator?

A flat battery and a failing alternator feel similar. The difference is that a charging fault keeps draining a good battery. Have the charging system tested so you replace the right part rather than guessing.
