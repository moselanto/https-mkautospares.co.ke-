Brakes are the one system you never gamble on. Knowing when to replace your brake pads, and what affects the cost, keeps you safe and saves money in the long run.

Signs your brake pads need replacing

  • A squealing or grinding noise when you brake.
  • Longer stopping distances or a soft pedal.
  • Vibration through the pedal or steering when braking.
  • A visible thin pad when you look through the wheel.
  • A brake warning light on the dashboard.

What affects brake pad life

City stop-start traffic, heavy loads, hilly routes, and hard braking all wear pads faster. Rear pads and front pads wear at different rates, and worn pads left too long will damage the discs, which is a far more expensive repair.

Genuine, aftermarket, or ex-Japan?

For brakes we recommend genuine or trusted aftermarket pads bought new. Discs (rotors) can sometimes be sourced ex-Japan in good condition, but friction material should always be fresh. Ask us and we will advise for your specific car.
