The Nissan X-Trail is a popular family SUV in Kenya, valued for space and comfort. High-mileage examples share a few common issues worth knowing before they become expensive.

Transmission (CVT)

Many X-Trails use a CVT that depends on clean fluid at the correct intervals. Hesitation, whining, or jerky drive can point to neglected fluid, so keep transmission servicing up to date.

Suspension and bushes

As an SUV on rough roads, the X-Trail wears shocks, control-arm bushes, and links. Knocking over bumps or vague steering are the usual clues.

Cooling system

Keep the radiator, thermostat, and coolant healthy to prevent overheating, especially in traffic and hot conditions.

Brakes and service items

Front brakes, along with air, oil, and cabin filters, are routine wear items. Staying ahead of them keeps the SUV smooth and reliable.
