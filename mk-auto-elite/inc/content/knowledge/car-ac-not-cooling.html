When your car's air conditioning blows warm air in Nairobi traffic or at the coast, driving becomes miserable. Most AC problems have simple causes. Here are the most common ones and how they are fixed.

Most common causes

  1. Low refrigerant (gas) from a slow leak. Topping up without fixing the leak only works for a short time.
  2. Blocked cabin filter, which cuts air flow. Cheap and easy to replace.
  3. Faulty compressor clutch or a worn compressor.
  4. Dirty or damaged condenser in front of the radiator.
  5. Cooling fan not working, so the AC cools well on the highway but not in traffic.
  6. Blown fuse or failed relay.
  7. Blocked expansion valve or a faulty pressure switch.

Quick checks you can do

  • Listen for a click from the engine bay when you switch the AC on. No click often means an electrical or compressor clutch problem.
  • Check that the radiator fans run when the AC is on.
  • Replace the cabin filter if it has not been changed in a year.

Prevention

Run the AC for a few minutes every week, even in cool weather, to keep the seals lubricated. Change the cabin filter at every second service.
