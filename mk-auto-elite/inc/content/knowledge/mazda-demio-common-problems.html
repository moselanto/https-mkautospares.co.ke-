The Mazda Demio is one of Kenya's favourite small cars: affordable, economical and easy to park. Like any car, it has a few known weak points, on both the older DE (2007-2014) and newer DJ (2014 onwards) models. Here are the problems we see most, and the parts that fix them.

1. Automatic gearbox changes

Older automatic Demios can give harsh or delayed gear changes when the transmission fluid is old. Regular fluid changes with the correct Mazda fluid help a lot. Ignoring early signs leads to expensive gearbox repairs.

2. Suspension and steering

Front stabilizer links, lower arm bushes and shocks wear on Kenyan roads, causing knocking and loose steering. Demio brake pads are affordable and quick to replace.

3. Engine misfires

Worn spark plugs and ignition coils cause rough idling, misfires and higher fuel use. Replace plugs on schedule and test the coils if the check engine light comes on. See spark plugs and ignition coils.

4. SkyActiv diesel soot build-up (DJ diesel)

Diesel Demios driven only short distances in town can build up soot in the DPF and intake. Regular highway drives and the correct low-ash diesel oil help prevent it.

5. Cooling system

Radiator leaks and tired thermostats on older Demios cause overheating. Replacing the radiator and coolant on time is far cheaper than an engine repair. See why cars overheat.

6. Battery and charging

Weak batteries and failing alternators show up as slow starting and dim lights. Test both together.
