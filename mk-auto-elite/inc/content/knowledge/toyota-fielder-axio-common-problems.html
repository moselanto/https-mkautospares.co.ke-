The Toyota Fielder and Axio (NZE 141/161 and hybrid NKE 165) are among the most common cars on Kenyan roads. They are reliable, but after years on local roads a few problems appear again and again. Here is what to watch for and which parts fix it.

1. Suspension knocks

Stabilizer links, stabilizer bushes, lower arm bushes and front shocks wear quickly on rough roads and speed bumps. A knock over bumps or a loose steering feel are the usual signs. These are low-cost repairs: see signs your suspension needs replacing.

2. CVT gearbox issues (newer models)

Many later Fielders and Axios use a CVT. Slipping, a whining noise or a delay when engaging drive often points to old or wrong fluid. Use only the Toyota CVT fluid specified for your car. Read our CVT gearbox guide.

3. High fuel consumption

Dirty air filters, worn spark plugs, a faulty oxygen sensor or a dirty throttle body make the engine thirsty. A proper service usually brings consumption back to normal. See how to reduce fuel consumption.

4. Hybrid battery faults (Fielder / Axio Hybrid)

Hybrid versions can show "Check Hybrid System" when the battery ages or its cooling fan is blocked with dust. See our hybrid battery price guide.

5. Engine mounts and vibration

Worn engine mounts cause vibration at idle, especially in Drive. Replacing the mounts restores a smooth ride.

6. Overheating

Old radiators, thermostats and water pumps can cause overheating in traffic. See why cars overheat.

Service parts to keep handy

  • Oil filter, air filter and cabin filter
  • Iridium spark plugs
  • Front brake pads
  • Stabilizer links and bushes
