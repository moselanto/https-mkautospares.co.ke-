Why the same part has different prices

  • Genuine (OEM) parts cost most and usually last longest.
  • Quality aftermarket parts from known brands are often 30 to 50 percent cheaper.
  • Ex-Japan used parts are good value for big items such as alternators, gearboxes and engines.
  • Very cheap copies are often fakes. Read genuine vs aftermarket vs ex-Japan before you choose.

How to get the best price without the wrong part

  1. Send your chassis number so the part is matched exactly. A wrong part costs you twice.
  2. Compare like with like: genuine with genuine, aftermarket with aftermarket.
  3. Ask about warranty and returns before paying.
  4. Ask about Lipa Mdogo Mdogo if the repair is large.
