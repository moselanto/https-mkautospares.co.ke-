Buying car parts in Kenya usually comes down to three options: brand-new genuine (OEM) parts, aftermarket parts, and ex-Japan (used, imported from Japan) parts. Each has its place. The trick is matching the part to the job and to your budget.

Genuine (OEM) parts

Genuine parts are made by, or for, your vehicle's manufacturer to the exact original specification. They offer the best fit, the longest life, and usually a warranty. They cost the most, and for safety-critical or hard-to-reach components they are often worth it.

Aftermarket parts

Aftermarket parts are made by third-party manufacturers and range from excellent to poor. Reputable brands can match or exceed OEM quality at a lower price, while cheap unbranded parts can fail early. Buy aftermarket from names your mechanic trusts, and be extra careful with braking, steering, and suspension components.

Ex-Japan (used) parts

Kenya's roads are full of Japanese imports, so low-mileage used parts pulled from Japanese vehicles are widely available and offer genuine OEM quality at a fraction of the new price. They are ideal for body panels, lights, electronics, gearboxes, and many mechanical assemblies. Always confirm the part was inspected and that it matches your chassis code.

A simple rule of thumb

  • Safety-critical wear parts (brake pads and discs): genuine or trusted aftermarket, bought new.
  • Expensive assemblies (gearboxes, alternators, headlights): ex-Japan is often excellent value.
  • Body and trim: ex-Japan is usually the smart choice.
