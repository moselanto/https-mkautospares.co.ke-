Privacy Policy

MK Auto Spares respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. It is aligned with the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya).

Information We Collect

  • Contact and order details you provide — name, phone number, email, delivery address, and the vehicle/parts you enquire about.
  • Payment information needed to complete a transaction (for example an M-Pesa confirmation code). We do not store full card details on our website.
  • Technical data collected automatically when you browse — IP address, device and browser type, and pages visited — via cookies and similar technologies.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries, confirm fitment, quote prices and process orders.
  • To arrange delivery and provide customer support, returns and warranty service.
  • To improve our website, catalogue and service.
  • To send you updates or offers where you have asked to receive them (you can opt out at any time).
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Sharing Your Information

We share personal data only as needed to run our business — for example with courier and delivery partners to fulfil your order, and with payment providers to process payments. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where required by law.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to keep your session working (such as remembering your selected vehicle and cart) and to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect site functionality.

Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data and limit access to authorised personnel.

Your Rights

Under Kenyan data protection law you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to request correction or deletion, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page.
